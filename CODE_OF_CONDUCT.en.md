# Code of conduct

This is a space for learning. We want it to welcome beginners before experts.

## What we commit to

- Respect in discussion: criticize ideas, not people.
- Patience with simple questions; we all started from zero.
- Honesty in crediting work and sources to their owners.
- Accepting correction gracefully.

## What we don't accept

- Insults, mockery or harassment of any kind.
- Discrimination based on gender, origin, religion, country or level of experience.
- Publishing private information about anyone without their permission.
- Advertising and spam.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to the repository maintainers at [support@mahsumaah.sa](mailto:support@mahsumaah.sa). Every report is handled in confidence, and we may remove offending content or ban its author from taking part.
